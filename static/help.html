Creator guide

Your first workspace

A platform administrator provisions your organization and gives its administrator a seven-day invitation. Accept it from the sign-in page, choose your own username and password, and sign in. Tenant administrators can invite more creators or admins. Never post invitation tokens publicly.


Make your first track

Open Music Lab. Choose Simple or Custom, enter a description and title, and optionally write or request original lyrics. Select an available engine. Local note synth creates real audio without AI; optional MusicGen creates short instrumentals after server setup. Speech narration is explicitly an AI-generated voice, not sung vocals.


Bring and shape your audio

Create a Library project, confirm your rights, and upload audio up to 20 MB or record your microphone over HTTPS. Open an audio version to crop, reverse, change pitch/speed, add fades and apply a basic effect. Rendered versions appear as new assets. Jobs shows progress and refunds internal credits on failure.


Arrange a studio session

Open Studio, add audio from the library, set clip starts, trims, durations, volume and pan. Use the note grid or keys to sketch a melody; the inspector changes velocity and duration. Save your session. Render notes to WAV, then add that audio to the arrangement for a combined mix. Export a full mix, selected range or individual audio tracks. Import/export standard MIDI. Web MIDI requires a supported browser and permission.


Create a music video

Open Music Video Lab or choose Make video from a track. Choose audio, description and orientation. Plan a storyline with AI or write scenes yourself. Edit each caption, shot direction and duration, upload images or video clips, and choose each resource. Generate images or enabled motion clips when appropriate. Save, review and approve before rendering. The local renderer assembles images/clips and a soundtrack; it does not invent motion from an uploaded photo or lip-sync a face.


Organize and share

Create workspaces for invited collaborators with viewer/editor access. Put songs in playlists and albums. Projects begin private. Public publishing exposes that project’s media in Explore; link-only sharing exposes it to anyone with the link. Publish member songs separately for a collection. Returning to private revokes the link. Check rights and personal content before publication. Allow remixing only when you intend to permit independent public-source copies.


Know the limits

Sung AI vocals, verified voice cloning, trained custom models and many advanced DAW controls are not implemented. Engine status shows what is configured. Model/voice quality and hardware permissions need live testing. Contact your tenant administrator for access, credits or support; operators must publish their support contact. This guide is not a promise of Suno/Sondo compatibility.
