Privacy notice — operator approval draft

Status

Draft dated 17 October 2026. Before production, the operator must publish its legal identity, privacy/support contact, jurisdiction, retention periods, hosting location and configured providers. This text describes application behavior, not a compliance certification.


Information processed

The app stores account names, password hashes, memberships, project prompts/lyrics, uploaded and generated media, storyboards, notes, internal credit usage, jobs, invitations and audit events. It uses a session cookie for authentication. The supplied frontend includes no advertising trackers.


Sharing and AI

Private projects are accessible to their owner, authorized workspace collaborators and tenant administrators. Server operators may separately access storage and backups. Public media is available to visitors; unlisted links are bearer access links. OpenAI receives submitted prompts/lyrics, narration text or image/video prompts for requested AI jobs. Text requests use store:false; this is not a guarantee of zero provider retention. Enabled model workers may download model weights from their publishers. Review any additional configured integrations separately.


Retention and requests

No automatic universal deletion schedule is implemented. Removing media retains accounting/audit/job records and may retain text results or backup copies. Authorized remix copies and downloaded media cannot be recalled. Ask your organization administrator and operating entity about access, correction, deletion, retention and incident reports. The operator must define verification procedures and applicable rights before production.


Security

Access controls, hashed passwords/tokens and CSRF checks are implemented. Operators must configure HTTPS, backups, patching, monitoring, incident response and appropriate access management. No absolute confidentiality or regulatory compliance guarantee is made.
