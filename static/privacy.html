Privacy notice — operator approval draft

Status

Draft dated 17 October 2026. Before production, the operator must publish its legal identity, privacy/support contact, jurisdiction, retention periods, hosting location and configured providers. This text describes application behavior, not a compliance certification.


Information processed

The app stores account names, password hashes, memberships, project prompts/lyrics, uploaded and generated media, storyboards, notes, internal credit usage, jobs, invitations and audit events. It uses a session cookie for authentication. The supplied frontend includes no advertising trackers.


Sharing and AI

Private projects are accessible to their owner, authorized workspace collaborators and tenant administrators. Server operators may separately access storage and backups. Public media is available to visitors; unlisted links are bearer access links. OpenAI receives submitted prompts/lyrics, narration text or image/video prompts for requested AI jobs. Text requests use store:false; this is not a guarantee of zero provider retention. Enabled model workers may download model weights from their publishers. Review any additional configured integrations separately.


Retention and requests

No automatic universal deletion schedule is implemented. Removing media retains accounting/audit/job records and may retain text results or backup copies. Authorized remix copies and downloaded media cannot be recalled. Ask your organization administrator and operating entity about access, correction, deletion, retention and incident reports. The operator must define verification procedures and applicable rights before production.


Security

Access controls, hashed passwords/tokens and CSRF checks are implemented. Operators must configure HTTPS, backups, patching, monitoring, incident response and appropriate access management. No absolute confidentiality or regulatory compliance guarantee is made.


YouTube API Services

When you connect a channel, this app uses YouTube API Services and requests upload and read access through Google OAuth. OAuth tokens are stored only in protected server storage. We store the channel identifier and title, imported video identifiers and titles, and upload progress to provide the features you request. Explicit uploads send your chosen MP4, title, description, visibility and audience to YouTube. Imported references do not download video or audio. Imported metadata and finished upload history are removed after 30 days by the scheduled cleanup. Connections expire locally after 30 days and can be reconnected. Disconnecting deletes local tokens and imported references and attempts to revoke Google access; it does not delete published YouTube videos. Operational job and audit records and protected backups can remain under the operator’s retention policy. No YouTube data is sold or used for advertising.

See the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access through Google account permissions as well as the YouTube screen’s Disconnect control. Contact the operating organization using its support contact for access or deletion requests; operators must publish that contact before enabling OAuth.

Billing and marketplace

Billing stores your workspace, order reference, gateway, package, amount, currency, credit allocation and verification status. Your selected payment provider receives your account email, order reference, amount and currency to create hosted checkout. Card and bank credentials are entered with the provider and are not collected by this application. Platform administrators can review billing records. Marketplace listing descriptions are visible to active signed-in tenants after approval. Moderators can inspect submitted text; purchased text is available to members of the purchasing workspace. Sales reports show credit earnings. Contact the operator for billing disputes, content complaints or data requests.
